abstract: >-
  We developed a unified multitask model based on the MultiPatchFormer architecture, which processes multimodal VR signals through dual patch projection branches for gaze and classification inputs. The model employs multiscale patch embeddings, cross-attention between gaze and classification pathways, channel attention, and transformer encoders to jointly predict continuous user gaze and classify reaction time, cognitive load (mental load and physical load).
